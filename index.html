1. Introduction

Welcome to Dino Runner ("we", "our", or "us"). Dino Runner is a 2D endless-runner mobile game developed and published by Vikrant Patil.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the Dino Runner application published on Google Play Store under the package name com.vik.dinorunner.

The game is built using Flutter and the Flame game engine. Players control dinosaurs, collect coins, use power-ups and unlock additional characters and environments.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is shared with third-party services and what privacy choices are available.

Our Privacy Commitment

Dino Runner does not require users to create an account to play. Gameplay progress and preferences are designed to be stored locally on your device. However, advertising and platform services may process certain information as described below.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Dino Runner does not require registration, sign-in or account creation. We do not intentionally request your name, telephone number or email address to allow you to play.

2.2 Gameplay Information

The game may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Game scores and high scores.
  • In-game coins and rewards.
  • Unlocked dinosaurs and environments.
  • Selected characters and environments.
  • Sound and music preferences.
  • Tutorial and onboarding completion.
  • Other gameplay settings and progress.
  • Local purchase-related settings, where applicable.

Gameplay information is stored using local device storage. We do not operate a game-progress account system.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

Third-party SDKs and services may automatically process information depending on the services enabled in the app. This may include:

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • Application version and technical diagnostics.
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP.
  • Advertising requests, impressions and clicks.
  • Rewarded advertisement completion events.
  • Crash reports and performance information.
  • Purchase transaction status, where applicable.

Dino Runner is not designed to collect precise GPS location for ordinary gameplay.

2.4 Advertising Identifiers

Advertising providers may use advertising identifiers and other technologies to deliver, measure, limit or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable laws, user consent and device settings.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Save and maintain gameplay progress.
  • Display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements.
  • Measure advertising performance and revenue.
  • Prevent invalid advertising activity and fraud.
  • Process and verify in-app purchases.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve stability.
  • Understand aggregated game usage.
  • Improve gameplay and application performance.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. However, certain advertising-related disclosures may qualify as sharing or targeted advertising under applicable privacy laws.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Gameplay Storage

Gameplay information such as coins, scores, unlocks and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

4.2 Third-Party Processing

Advertising, analytics and platform services may process information on their own systems under their respective privacy policies. We do not control every aspect of their independent processing practices.

4.3 Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the game. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
