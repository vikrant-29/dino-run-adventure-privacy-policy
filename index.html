📋 1. Introduction

Welcome to Dino Run Adventure ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web game (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

🔒 Our Commitment: We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your gaming experience is safe and secure.

📊 2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our App. However, we may collect:

  • Game Progress Data: High scores, coins collected, and game settings stored locally on your device.
  • Device Information: Basic device information such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our App, we may automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, including game sessions, features used, and time spent.
  • Advertising ID: Your Android Advertising ID is used for serving personalized ads (you can opt out in your device settings).
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash data to help us improve App stability.

2.3 Local Storage Data

All game data is stored locally on your device using HTML5 localStorage:

  • High scores
  • Total coins collected
  • Game settings (sound, music, difficulty)
  • Onboarding completion status

🎯 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and Maintain the App: Ensure the App functions properly and improve user experience.
  • Personalize Your Experience: Save your game progress, high scores, and preferences.
  • Serve Advertisements: Display relevant ads through advertising partners.
  • Analyze Usage: Understand how users interact with the App to improve features.

🔒 4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store personal information on external servers.

4.2 Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  • Data stored locally on your device
  • No transmission of personal data over networks
  • No third-party access to your game data

🔗 5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may collect data as per their privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Policy

5.2 Analytics

We may use analytics services to improve the App. These services collect anonymous usage data.

📝 Note: We do not share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

⚖️ 6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: You can opt-out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • Clear Data: You can clear all local game data at any time.
  • Data Access: You can access your game data through the local storage.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all game data by clearing your browser cache.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
