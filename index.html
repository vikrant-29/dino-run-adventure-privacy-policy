📋 1. Introduction

Welcome to Dino Run Adventure ("we," "our," or "us"). Dino Run Adventure is a 2D endless-runner game built with Flutter and the Flame game engine, in which you guide a dinosaur through Desert, City, Sea Side, Railway Station, and Jungle environments, collect coins and power-ups, and unlock additional playable dinosaurs and environments. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use Dino Run Adventure on Android, iOS, or the web (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please do not download, access, or use the App.

🔒 Our Commitment: Dino Run Adventure has no user accounts, no chat or social features, and no servers of our own. Nearly everything about your play session - your progress, unlocks, and settings - stays on your own device.

📊 2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Dino Run Adventure does not require registration, sign-in, or any personal information to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number at any point in the App.

2.2 Gameplay Data Stored on Your Device

To let you pick up where you left off, the App saves the following directly on your device (not on our servers), using Flutter's standard local-storage plugin (Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults, or your browser's local storage if you play the web version):

  • Score data: your current run's score and your all-time high score.
  • Coins: your total in-game coin balance, earned by playing.
  • Unlocks: which environments (e.g. Jungle, Desert, City, Sea Side, Railway Station) and which playable dinosaurs (e.g. T-Rex, Triceratops, Velociraptor, Stegosaurus, Ankylosaurus, Parasaurolophus, Spinosaurus, Carnotaurus) you've unlocked with coins.
  • Selections: which dinosaur and environment you last chose to play.
  • Preferences: your sound-effects and music on/off settings.
  • App state: whether you've completed the onboarding/tutorial screens, and whether you've purchased the optional "Remove Ads" upgrade (see Section 7).

None of this gameplay data is transmitted to us, and we cannot see it, back it up, or recover it if you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

Some information may be collected automatically by us or by the third-party services described in Section 5, strictly to operate the App, serve ads, and keep it stable:

  • Device & diagnostic information: device model, operating system version, app version, and general performance/crash diagnostics.
  • Advertising identifiers: your Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), used by our ad provider to serve and measure ads. On iOS this only happens if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Ad and purchase interaction events: e.g. that an ad was shown, closed, or watched to completion, or that a purchase was completed - handled by Google/Apple as described below.

🎯 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Run the game: save your progress, unlocks, and settings locally so they persist between sessions.
  • Show ads: display banner ads, interstitial ads between runs, and optional rewarded video ads (see Section 6).
  • Process purchases: unlock the "Remove Ads" upgrade after a successful purchase through Google Play or the App Store.
  • Maintain and improve the App: diagnose crashes and performance issues and understand, in aggregate, how the App is used.

We do not use your information to build an advertising profile of our own, and we do not sell your information.

🔒 4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local-First Storage

Your gameplay data (Section 2.2) is stored only on your device. We do not operate a backend server or database that stores your game progress, and we cannot access it remotely.

4.2 Data Security

Because most data never leaves your device, the practical security measures that matter most are:

  • Gameplay data stays within the App's private, sandboxed storage area on your device.
  • No account credentials exist for Dino Run Adventure, so there is nothing of that kind to be breached.
  • Any data that is transmitted off-device (ad requests, purchase validation) is handled by Google or Apple over their own encrypted, secure channels - see Section 5.

🔧 5. App Permissions

Dino Run Adventure requests only the permissions it needs to function:

Permission Why it's needed
Internet access Loading ads, verifying in-app purchases, and (optionally) sharing your score.
Network state Checking connectivity before requesting an ad, so the game doesn't stall offline.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, or files, and does not need any such permission to play.

🔗 6. Advertising & Third-Party Services

6.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to show:

  • Banner ads displayed on menu and gameplay screens.
  • Interstitial ads shown occasionally between runs (for example, after every few game-overs).
  • Rewarded video ads that you may choose to watch in order to revive and continue a run. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional and declining one never blocks you from continuing to the game-over screen and starting a new run.

AdMob may collect and process data (such as your advertising identifier and approximate, non-precise location derived from IP address) under Google's own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use AdMob partner sites or apps

On iOS, personalized/tracking-based ads only occur if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, just not personalized to your device's advertising identifier. You can also opt out of personalized advertising generally in your device's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking / Apple Advertising).

6.2 Sharing & Utility Plugins

The App uses a small number of standard Flutter plugins that do not collect or transmit data to us:

  • Share functionality: if you choose to share your score, this opens your device's native share sheet; whatever you send and to whom is entirely your choice and passes through the apps you select, not through us.
  • Link opening: used to open external links (such as this policy or store listings) in your browser.
  • App info: reads your installed app version/build number locally, purely for display (e.g. in Settings) - this is not transmitted anywhere.

📝 Note: We do not share your information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes. The only outside parties involved are Google (advertising and, where applicable, Play Billing) and Apple (App Store purchases), each acting under their own privacy policies.

💳 7. In-App Purchases

Dino Run Adventure offers one optional in-app purchase: Remove Ads, a one-time purchase that turns off banner and interstitial ads.

  • All purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store, depending on your device.
  • We never see or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details - that information is handled exclusively by Google/Apple.
  • Your purchase status ("ads removed: yes/no") is saved locally on your device, the same way as your other gameplay settings.
  • In-game coins are earned only by playing (collecting them during runs and spending them to unlock dinosaurs/environments) - they are not sold as a purchasable currency.

For purchase issues, refunds, or billing questions, please contact Google Play or Apple support directly, as we do not have access to your payment information to resolve these on our end.

⚖️ 8. Your Rights and Choices

Because your gameplay data lives on your device rather than on our servers, you are always in direct control of it:

  • Access: your high score, coins, and unlocks are visible at any time within the App itself.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage/cache from your device's app settings, permanently deletes all locally-stored gameplay data.
  • Opt-out of personalized ads: use your device's ad-personalization settings (see Section 6.1), or purchase Remove Ads to stop seeing ads entirely.
  • Regional privacy rights: depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) - for example, the right to request details of any data a third-party ad partner holds about your device. Since we hold no server-side personal data ourselves, most such requests should be directed to Google (our ad and billing partner) via the links in Section 6.1, and we're glad to help point you in the right direction if you contact us at the email below.

👶 9. Children's Privacy

Dino Run Adventure is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no chat, messaging, user profiles, or user-generated content of any kind.

If our App is made available on a children's platform or through a "Families"-style program in the future, advertising on that build will be configured to serve only non-personalized, contextual ads appropriate for that audience, consistent with Google Play Families Policy and Apple's Kids Category requirements. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in Section 11.
